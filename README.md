Solara Bay is an original, offline top-down crime game set in a sunlit procedural coastal city. Steal cars, outrun patrols, and deliver three jobs across the bay. Move with WASD, aim with the mouse, use E to enter or leave cars, and press T or R to start side work.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser to play.

## How to play

- Move or drive with WASD; hold Shift to sprint on foot.
- Press E to enter or leave a car. Aim with the mouse and attack with the left mouse button or Space; press F to punch.
- Take on the story jobs in order: Borrowed Time, followed by the remaining jobs as they unlock. Press T to start a taxi fare or R to start a rampage.
- Press P or Esc to pause, and M to mute.
